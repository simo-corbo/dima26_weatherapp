The contract — build to this, exactly

The mockups above show the design assembled. The spec below is the source of truth: every colour, size, radius, and layout rule your project must match. Each component maps 1:1 to a widget.

§1

What the app is

A small weather app on a blue “sky” background with frosted-glass cards. Four screens: Home, Saved Cities, Detail + Forecast, Settings. Data comes from the OpenWeather API. The visual language is calm, airy, single-accent. The design does not change as the app grows — only the behaviour behind it.

§6

Components

each = one widget you build

GlassCard

Frosted container: ClipRRect + backdrop blur 14 + Container fill glass.fill, border glass.stroke. Defaults radius 22, padding 20. Variants override radius/fill (chips: radius 14, fill 10%).

SearchField

GlassCard (radius 14, fill 10%, padding h14): search icon (18, white 85%) + text input. Placeholder “Search city” (ink.hint). Submit runs a search.

WeatherIcon

Maps a condition to a glyph (see §8). Clear → sun colour; all others → white.

InfoChip

GlassCard (radius 14, fill 10%, padding h13 v9): Row[ icon 16 (white 90%) · gap 7 · Column(value, label) ]. Used for Humidity and Wind.

WeatherCard · Home

Centred Column: city (15/600) + save heart (18; filled & sun when saved, outline white 80% when not) · gap 8 · WeatherIcon 96 · temp (38/200) · description (13, white 78%) · gap 16 · Row[ humidity chip · gap 10 · wind chip ].

CityRow · Saved

GlassCard (radius 18, padding h15 v13): Row[ WeatherIcon 30 · gap 13 · Expanded Column(city 15/600, description 12 secondary) · temp 18/300 · gap 6 · chevron white 58% ]. Whole row tappable → Detail.

DayCard · Detail strip

Expanded GlassCard (radius 16, fill 16% if today else 10%, padding v13 h4): Column(weekday or “Today” 11/600 white 78% · glyph 26 · max 15/600 · min 12 secondary).

TabBar

Centred Row of two items (icon 22 + label 11/600). Active = full opacity; inactive = 55%. Sits in the bottom safe area.

Segmented · Settings

Pill padded 3, fill 10%, stroke, radius 11. Option padded h12 v5, radius 8; selected = white fill + sky.mid text; unselected = white 78%.

Toggle · Settings

46×28 pill. ON: white track 92%, knob sky.mid. OFF: track white 10% + stroke, knob white. Animate ~160 ms.

BackButton

34×34 glass square (radius 12, fill 10%, stroke) with a chevron-left.

§10

Data source — OpenWeather

GET /data/2.5/weather?q={city}&units=metric&appid={key}

Read name · main.temp · main.feels_like · main.humidity · weather[0].main · weather[0].description · wind.speed (m/s × 3.6 = km/h). For location, use &lat=&lon= instead of q.

GET /data/2.5/forecast?q={city}&units=metric&appid={key}

3-hourly list → aggregate into 5 days (min/max + midday condition). All fetched metric (°C); the °C/°F toggle converts at display time (no refetch). Throw on non-200 → error state. Key stays a placeholder YOUR_OPENWEATHER_API_KEY.

§11

App-wide units

one setting, every temperature

Store temperatures in °C. A single formatTemp(context, celsius) reads the settings state and returns °C or °F. Every temperature widget (WeatherCard, CityRow, DayCard, Detail hero) calls it, so flipping the unit in Settings rebuilds all temperatures at once.
